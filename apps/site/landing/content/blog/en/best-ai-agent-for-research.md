For most people in 2026, Perplexity Pro is the best default AI research agent — it returns a cited report in a few minutes and has the lowest citation-failure rate among the tools compared. ChatGPT Deep Research and Gemini Deep Research are strong alternates, each winning on a different axis: depth, or integration with your Google account.

## What a research agent actually does

A research agent isn't a chatbot you interrogate question by question. You give it one open-ended brief — "compare the three leading electric SUVs on reliability, price, and real-world range" — and it runs a loop: searches the web, reads the pages, cross-checks, and hands you a structured report with sources. The whole point is that it does the *browsing* so you don't spend an afternoon in tabs.

That loop is the same plan-act-check pattern as any [personal AI agent](https://wolffi.sh/blog/what-is-a-personal-ai-agent) — the difference is that research agents are pointed at the open web and built to cite their work.

Treat any single percentage as directional — tools move fast — but the *shape* of the trade-off has been stable for months.

## How to pick

Match the tool to the job, not the brand:

- **"Answer me fast, with receipts"** → Perplexity. It's the best all-rounder when you need a cited answer before lunch.
- **"Dig into something genuinely complicated"** → ChatGPT Deep Research. Its reports are longer and it reasons harder, at the cost of time.
- **"Research that involves my email and files"** → Gemini Deep Research, because it can pull from [your Google account](https://wolffi.sh/start#research) alongside the web — unique, and the reason to pick it if you live in Workspace.

## When a research agent beats a search engine

A research agent isn't always the right tool. Use it when:

1. The question has no single obvious page — it's a *synthesis*, not a lookup.
2. You need sources you can hand to someone else, not vibes.
3. The answer spans many pages and you'd otherwise open ten tabs.

Use a plain search (or just ask your [always-available assistant](https://wolffi.sh/start#research)) when the answer is a fact: a price, a date, a definition. Spinning up a deep-research run for "what's the capital of Norway" is like sending a drone for the mail.

## Takeaway

Perplexity Pro is the best default research agent for most people, ChatGPT Deep Research wins on depth, and Gemini Deep Research wins if your research lives in Google's apps. The durable lesson: research agents have gotten good enough that "go look this up and come back with sources" is now a task you can genuinely delegate — which is exactly the kind of job [an agent is for](https://wolffi.sh/blog/what-is-a-personal-ai-agent).
