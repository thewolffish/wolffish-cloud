An AI email agent reads your inbox and does the repetitive work for you — sorting, summarizing, drafting replies, and (with your approval) sending them. The trick is automating the busywork without handing over the final say, and this guide walks you through exactly how to set that up.

## What an AI email agent can actually do

Not every task is a good candidate for automation. The ones that are:

- **Triage** — sort incoming mail into "needs reply," "read later," "unsubscribe," and "noise."
- **Summarize** — turn a 20-message thread into three sentences and a recommendation.
- **Draft replies** — write a response in your voice for you to approve and send.
- **Flag the urgent** — surface the one email that matters before it scrolls off.
- **Schedule** — pause your inbox, batch replies, and remind you to follow up.

The tasks to keep human are anything with money, sensitive recipients, or a tone you can't get wrong.

## A safe setup in five steps

1. **Pick where it runs.** A cloud email client with AI built in (Shortwave, Missive, Superhuman) is the fastest start. For more control, run your own agent that connects to your inbox — [compare the trade-offs here](https://wolffi.sh/blog/local-first-vs-cloud-ai-assistant).
2. **Connect read-only first.** Give the agent the ability to read your inbox, not send. Watch what it does before you let it act.
3. **Teach it your rules.** Write down your triage rules — who's important, what's spam, what gets a fast reply. Explicit rules beat "be smart about it."
4. **Start with summaries, not sends.** Have it produce a daily digest of what needs attention and draft replies for you to approve.
5. **Turn on sending only with approval.** The last thing you enable is autonomous sending, and only for the narrow cases you've already verified.

This mirrors how [independent reviews of AI email assistants](https://missiveapp.com/blog/ai-email-assistant) describe the mature workflow: the AI drafts and organizes, the human decides what actually goes out.

## What to keep human, always

- Anything involving **money**, **contracts**, or **legal** implications.
- Replies to **sensitive recipients** — a boss, a client, a difficult conversation.
- Messages where **tone is the whole point** — apologies, negotiations, bad news.

AI is good at being clear and fast; it is not good at knowing when the right move is to be human. Keep that line for yourself.

## Takeaway

Email automation is the single highest-leverage place to start with an AI agent, because it's repetitive, measurable, and easy to supervise. Start read-only, write your rules down, and let the agent draft while you keep the send button.
