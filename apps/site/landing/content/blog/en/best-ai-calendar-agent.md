The best AI calendar agent in 2026 is Reclaim if you want focus time protected on a budget, and Motion if you want tasks, meetings, and notes in one paid platform. Both actually move events on your calendar — that is what makes them agents, not reminders. Clockwise, the old third option, shut down in March 2026 after the team joined Salesforce, so the choice is now two products with opposite philosophies.

## What an AI calendar agent actually does

A scheduling link is not an agent. An agent watches your week, places work into open slots, and reshuffles when a meeting lands on a deep-work block. You stop dragging events around by hand.

Two designs dominate:

- **Defend habits.** You name recurring blocks ("Deep work 9–11"). The tool protects them and proposes other times for meetings. That is Reclaim.
- **Schedule from deadlines.** You dump tasks with due dates. The tool builds the week backwards from Friday's deliverable and rebuilds it when the calendar changes. That is Motion.

Pick the design that matches how your week actually works, not the one with the prettier app.

## Reclaim: the integration specialist

[Reclaim's pricing page](https://www.reclaim.ai/pricing) is unusually clear. Lite is free. Starter is $10/month on annual billing ($12 monthly). Business is $15 annual ($18 monthly). Enterprise is $22 annual.

What you get for that:

- Habits — recurring blocks the AI defends instead of deleting when a meeting appears
- Sync from Asana, ClickUp, Todoist, Jira, Linear, and Google Tasks, so tasks you already track become calendar time
- Smart Meetings and scheduling links on paid plans
- iOS and Android apps, Google Calendar and Outlook

Reclaim was acquired by Dropbox in August 2024 and kept the same product thesis: do not replace your project tool, sit next to it. The free Lite plan is real — one habit, one week of scheduling, one user — which is enough to test whether habit defense changes your week.

Honest limits: no native meeting transcription (pair it with Otter or Fireflies), occasional Outlook sync lag, and no org-wide "no-meeting Friday" enforcement. It optimizes *your* calendar, not the company's culture.

## Motion: the all-in-one

[Motion's pricing](https://www.usemotion.com/pricing) starts at $19 per seat per month for Pro AI (about $12.73 on annual billing, a 33% cut). Business AI is $29 / ~$19.43. There is a 7-day trial and no permanent free tier.

Motion replaces the stack: calendar, tasks, projects, AI meeting notes, docs, and sheets. The useful trick is cross-component intelligence — a Friday deadline books Wednesday afternoon, and action items from the notetaker land on the task list. Calendar scheduling itself is algorithmic and does not burn the plan's AI credits; those credits are for generative features.

Honest limits: you migrate into Motion's world. Independent reviews flag painful export and a steeper learning curve. If your team lives in Jira or Asana and will not move, you will double-enter work. Five people on Business AI annual is roughly $145/month versus Reclaim Business at about $75/month for the same headcount — the gap is the price of consolidation.

## Which one should you pick

- **You already like Asana, ClickUp, Todoist, Jira, or Linear** → Reclaim. It reads those tools. Motion does not.
- **You want one app and will migrate** → Motion.
- **You are testing on $0** → Reclaim Lite. Motion has no free plan.
- **Meetings eat the day and you need transcripts** → Motion, or Reclaim plus a notetaker.
- **ADHD / decision fatigue on "when do I do this"** → Motion's auto-placement of tasks is the better match. Reclaim's repeating habits help if structure, not novelty, is what you need.

Do not run both. Two agents moving the same events will fight.

A personal agent that *talks* to your calendar — "what's Thursday look like, move the call to 4" — is a different product. The [calendar setup on wolffi.sh/start](https://wolffi.sh/start#calendar) is that pattern: you message the agent, it acts on Google Calendar with your approval. Reclaim and Motion sit *inside* the calendar and rearrange it without a chat. Many people end up with both layers: an optimizer on the calendar, and a personal agent you text.

## Takeaway

Reclaim is the default for 2026: a real free tier, it respects the tools you already pay for, and habit defense is the feature most calendars still fake. Pay for Motion when you are ready to throw out the rest of the stack and let deadlines drive the week. Either way, stop dragging blocks by hand — that is the whole point of an agent.
