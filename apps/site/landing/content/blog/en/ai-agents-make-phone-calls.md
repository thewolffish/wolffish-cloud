AI agents can now make phone calls for you. Google is rolling out agentic calling — a tool that dials a local store, asks about a product, checks stock and sales, then texts you the answer. It's part of a wider shift where agents no longer just browse for you; they pick up the phone. Here's what these agents actually do, where they're available, and how to use them without handing over control.

## The news: agents moved from typing to talking

In a suite of AI shopping features announced ahead of the 2026 holiday season, Google added "Let Google Call" — an agent you direct to phone local businesses and ask what you'd want to know. The [announcement coverage](https://www.theverge.com/news/819431/google-shopping-ai-gemini-agentic-checkout-calling) describes the full picture:

- **Agentic calling** — the agent calls a store, asks about products, what's in stock, and whether there are sales or promotions.
- **Disclosure built in** — the agent tells the store it's an AI caller, and merchants can opt out if they don't want to field robot calls.
- **Results delivered to you** — after the call, you get a text or email with the information gathered.
- **Rollout by category** — starting with toys, health and beauty, and electronics.

That's the same "calling gap" that's been closing all year: the friction of a phone call was the last thing an agent couldn't do. Now, for a growing set of tasks, it can.

## What an agent that calls can actually do

The useful part isn't the novelty of a robot dialing — it's the class of tasks it unlocks. These are the jobs that used to require you to sit on hold or drive to a shop:

1. **Check availability** — is this item in stock at the store near me?
2. **Confirm pricing and promos** — what's the real price, and is there a discount I'd miss online?
3. **Ask a specific question** — does this service cover my area, my model, my case?
4. **Then act on it** — with agentic checkout, the agent can even complete a purchase once you approve.

This is enormously useful for people who can't or don't want to spend a morning calling around. For a [personal agent](https://wolffi.sh/start), adding a phone capability means the assistant can handle research *and* the human step.

## Where it stands right now

Agentic calling and agentic checkout aren't everywhere yet — they're rolling out, starting in certain categories and regions. What's genuinely new is the pattern: an agent can transact across a physical world that has no API. No integration, no developer access — just a voice and a phone number. That's the same reason [computer-use agents](https://wolffi.sh/blog/qwen-ui-agent-computer-use) matter: they work where there's no clean interface to hook into.

## How to use it safely

The power here is real, and so is the surface. Three rules keep you in control:

1. **Keep the agent on the read-and-report side first.** Let it call, gather, and text you back before it ever buys anything. You stay the decision-maker.
2. **Set the price before it acts.** Agentic checkout works off a limit you set — the agent pings you only if the item dips below it. Set that threshold deliberately, because the agent won't negotiate past it.
3. **Expect it to disclose.** The agent tells the store it's an AI. That's a feature — it means you're not running an anonymous bot that misrepresents itself, and merchants who don't want calls can opt out.

The same [security-first habits](https://wolffi.sh/blog/ai-agent-security) that apply to any capable agent apply here: an agent that can talk and spend is an agent to supervise. Start read-only, then grant spending only after you trust the pattern.

## Takeaway

Agents that make phone calls close the last gap between digital and real. Google's agentic calling dials stores, checks stock and pricing, and reports back — and agentic checkout can finish the purchase on a limit you set. It's a real capability, rolling out by category, not a universal assistant you should hand your whole life to yet. Use it to save the tedious phone calls, keep approvals in your hands, and you'll get the benefit without giving up control.
