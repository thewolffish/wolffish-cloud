SpaceX now owns Cursor, the most popular AI coding agent, after closing a $60 billion all-stock acquisition — the largest purchase of a venture-backed startup in history. The deal matters to everyone, not just developers, because it marks the moment AI agents stopped being features and became the main event of the tech industry.

## The deal, in plain terms

- SpaceX, through its SpaceXAI arm, bought Anysphere — the company behind Cursor — for $60 billion in stock, [per Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacex-buy-anysphere-60-billion-2026-06-16/).
- The agreement was first announced in June 2026 and closed on August 14, 2026, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/spacex-completes-its-60-billion-cursor-acquisition).
- It is widely described as the largest acquisition of a venture-backed startup ever.

Cursor is an AI coding agent: software that writes, edits, and explains code inside your editor, and increasingly acts on your behalf across a codebase. It became the clearest example of an "agent" people actually pay for and depend on every day.

## Why a rocket company bought a code editor

SpaceX is not buying a code editor; it's buying an agent business with real, paying users and a distribution channel into every developer's workflow. Cursor gives SpaceXAI a product to anchor its push against Anthropic and OpenAI, where agents — not chatbots — are the battleground.

Coding was always going to be the beachhead. It's measurable (does the code work?), it's high-value, and it's where the "agent" framing first proved people will pay a subscription for an assistant that does the work rather than one that just answers questions.

## What it signals for the agent market

Three things stand out:

- **Agents are the new platform.** The same way browsers, then phones, consolidated value, the agent layer is now where the money is concentrating.
- **Coding first, everything else next.** The agent pattern that works in code — plan, act, check, report — is spreading to email, calendar, writing, and daily tasks.
- **Independent tools get absorbed.** The biggest independent agent product just became part of a larger empire. Expect more consolidation.

| Signal | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| $60B for a single agent product | Agents are now the center of gravity, not a side feature |
| A non-AI giant did the buying | Every large company wants an agent play |
| Coding led the way | Measurable tasks prove value first, then spread everywhere |
