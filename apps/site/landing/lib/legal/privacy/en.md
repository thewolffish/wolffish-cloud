This Privacy Policy describes how Wolffish ("Wolffish", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Wolffish desktop and mobile applications (the "Apps") and the wolffi.sh website (the "Site"). Wolffish is built local-first: your content lives on your device, and we collect as little as possible.

## Information We Collect

**Information stored on your device.** Your conversations, memories, files, skills, and settings — including any API keys you add — are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us, and we have no access to it.

**Information you provide to us.** If you contact us (for example by email), we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

**Technical information.** When the Apps check for updates or when you visit the Site, our servers and hosting providers may automatically log standard technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, operating system, and request timestamps. We use these logs for security, debugging, and keeping the service running.

We do not sell personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in the Apps.

## How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to:

- Provide, operate, and improve the Apps and the Site
- Deliver updates and new releases
- Respond to questions and support requests
- Maintain security and prevent abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## Device Permissions

The Apps may request operating-system permissions — such as file and storage access, network access, notifications, microphone, or camera — only when needed to perform tasks you initiate. You can review and revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

If you connect Wolffish to a third-party AI model provider, the prompts and content you choose to send are transmitted to that provider and handled under its own privacy policy. When you use local models, your content stays on your device.

The Apps may be distributed through marketplaces such as the Apple App Store and Google Play, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies. The Site and our release infrastructure run on hosting providers that process technical data on our behalf.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only:

- With service providers that host our infrastructure, acting on our instructions
- When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
- To protect the rights, safety, and security of Wolffish, our users, or the public

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally in the Apps remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Apps. Server logs are retained for a limited period and then deleted or anonymized. To request deletion of any information we hold about you (such as email correspondence), contact us at the address below.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing or withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

The Apps and the Site are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised version on this page and update the date at the top. Material changes may also be announced through the Apps or the Site.
