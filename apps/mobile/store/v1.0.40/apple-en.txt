Come back to the app while something is still running on your desktop and the reply is where you left it. Reopening a conversation mid-run used to show only the thinking words — the reply so far, the tool cards and any open question gone until the next update, which across a long tool call can be minutes away. The turn is now restored the moment the app reconnects, including a question or approval the run is waiting on.

Long conversations no longer drop the connection. A transcript heavy with tool work could outgrow what a single message on the link carries, and opening it took the link down every time. Oversized transcripts now arrive in pieces and are put back together on the phone.

The chat feed stays at the end of a reply as it arrives — it no longer stops short partway up, and the newest message no longer hides behind the composer when the keyboard rises.

The chevron that puts the keyboard away now appears above every keyboard in the app, not just the message field: settings fields, dialogs, search lines and the full-screen editors. One tap drops the keys and keeps everything you typed.
